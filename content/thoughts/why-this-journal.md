I write in public on X as [0xluckywiz](https://x.com/0xluckywiz). That is a good medium for a pulse. It is a bad medium for a thesis.

This site is the longer room.

I am Lucky Ajekevwoda. I sign the work **lucky kevwoda** — a shorter name, easier to carry, still mine. I am building [MoyoPay](https://moyopay.io), organising Logos Circle, and spending most of my attention on a single problem dressed in different clothes: how to stand up institutions that work for people here.

## Two shelves

**Research** is for the pieces I am willing to argue. Working notes on money rails, community as infrastructure, growth systems that do not insult the ground they stand on.

**Thoughts** is for the rest: field notes, doubts, things I learned this month and might revise next month.

I will publish when I have something with a spine, not when an algorithm is hungry. If you want a subscription, use the [RSS feed](/rss.xml). This is a journal, not a newsletter with a growth hack attached.

If you are building in public too — especially from a city that does not usually get the headline — write to me on X. I read.
