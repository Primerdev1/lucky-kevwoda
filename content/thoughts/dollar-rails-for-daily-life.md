A sentence I hear constantly on the timeline: *I have crypto… now what?*

That is the product gap. Not another wallet. Not another tutorial on seed phrases. A way for digital dollars to enter the same week as food, data, rent, and the cousin who needs ₦20,000 before Friday.

## The rail, not the ticker

Stablecoins already move through Africa in serious volume. People hold USDT and USDC because the naira, like many local currencies, asks them to run just to stay in place. Holding is a hedge. Using is still a maze: P2P chokepoints, five apps, a card that may or may not clear, a bill that still wants a bank.

MoyoPay is the attempt to collapse that maze into one balance. Save in USDT/USDC. Pay local bills. Send money. Spend on a card. The point is not to make someone “more onchain.” The point is to make the dollar they already reached for **behave like money**.

I co-founded it with my brother for a selfish and a public reason. The selfish one: we live this problem. The public one: a financial operating system that only works for people who already speak protocol is not an operating system. It is a club.

## Daily economic activity is the test

I do not trust a money product until it survives a boring Tuesday.

- Can someone pay a bill without performing expertise?
- Can value move to a person who has never opened a block explorer?
- Does the balance still mean something when the timeline is in a panic?
- Are the fiat edges actually licensed, or are we renting someone else’s risk?

Those questions are operational, legal, and cultural. They are also the research. Token design that cannot touch a light bill is a paper.

## Waitlists as a lab

We crossed a thousand people waiting before launch. A waitlist is easy to screenshot and hard to honour. The research practice I am trying to keep is to treat those names as a lab: talk to them, let them into the building, keep small promises, write down what they actually do with money — not what a persona slide says they should want.

If parallel institutions are the long thesis, dollar rails are the first load-bearing beam. People will not join a new economy they cannot pay for lunch inside.

I will keep publishing what we learn as the rails get real: what broke, what people needed that we did not expect, and where “DollarOS for Africa” is still just a sentence.
